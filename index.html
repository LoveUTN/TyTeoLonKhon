Tý Tẹo Lớn Khôn Publisher

An internal tool that publishes Tý Tẹo Lớn Khôn's own educational videos for children and parents to its own YouTube channel and Facebook Page.

What it does

Tý Tẹo Lớn Khôn produces short animated videos that teach children aged 3 to 12, and their parents, about body awareness, personal boundaries and staying safe. This tool saves the team from uploading every video by hand: once a video is ready, the tool sends it, together with its title, description and visibility setting, to the channel's YouTube account and Facebook Page.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Tý Tẹo Lớn Khôn channel. The tool has no public sign-up, no user accounts and no audience-facing features. It acts on a single YouTube channel and a single Facebook Page, both owned by the operator.

How it uses the YouTube API Services

The tool uses the YouTube Data API v3 for one purpose: uploading videos to the channel owner's own YouTube channel.

  1. One-time authorizationThe channel owner signs in with Google and grants the youtube.upload permission.
  2. UploadThe tool calls videos.insert with the video file and its metadata.
  3. DoneThe video appears on the channel. The tool reads no other channel data, comments, analytics or subscriber information.

By using this tool, the channel owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
